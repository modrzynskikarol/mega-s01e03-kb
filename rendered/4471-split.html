  • parser – the shared parser reads every date month first; tenants that write the day first get flipped dates.
  • hint – the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, so day-first tenants type month first. Form channel only; fix the hint, not the parser.
  • oneDay – dates off by exactly one day (a Tuesday shown as Monday, "only June", Lisbon and Toronto getting different days). That is not a swap. Timezone handling is the suspect.

How the causes were told apart

The cause depends on the tenant's locale and booking form, not on intake's tag, which is only a guess from the browser.
